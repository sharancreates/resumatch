<objective>
Implement the backend anonymous scan endpoint (`POST /api/analyze/public`) allowing students to evaluate resumes without creating an account or logging in, enforce a 3-scan quota per client IP/session, and provide a privacy-safe public scorecard generator (`GET /api/scorecards/{id}`).

Purpose: The primary student acquisition hook is an instant, frictionless free scan on the landing page. Requiring sign-up before showing value causes 70%+ visitor drop-off.
Output: Working public scan endpoint, IP rate limiter, shareable scorecard service, and test coverage.
</objective>
