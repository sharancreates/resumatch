    1. **Student Pricing Cards**:
       - **Free (₹0)**: 3 instant scans, match score, top 3 skill gaps, 1 standard template.
       - **Sprint Pass (₹199 / 14 days)** [⭐ Recommended / Most Popular badge]: Unlimited scans, grounded bullet rewriter, 1-click job tailor, 4 fresher ATS templates, PDF & DOCX exports.
       - **Placement Season Pass (₹499 / 3 months)** [🎓 Best for Campus Drives]: All Sprint Pass features for the entire placement semester, cover letter generator, priority queue.
       - **Campus License Banner**: "Are you a college TPO or student club lead? Institutional licenses available from ₹99/student."
    2. **Razorpay UPI Checkout Integration**:
       - Helper `loadRazorpayScript()`: Dynamically loads `https://checkout.razorpay.com/v1/checkout.js`.
       - On "Buy Pass" click:
         - Calls `POST /api/billing/order` with `{ plan_id }`.
         - If response is `status === "mock"`, prompts confirmation and executes mock upgrade with instant activation.
         - If response contains `order_id`:
           - Initializes `window.Razorpay` with options:
             - `key`: `response.data.key_id`
             - `amount`: `response.data.amount`
             - `currency`: "INR"
             - `order_id`: `response.data.order_id`
             - `name`: "ResuMatch AI"
             - `description`: "Student Access Pass"
             - `method`: `{ upi: true, card: true, netbanking: true }`
             - `handler`: Sends `{ razorpay_order_id, razorpay_payment_id, razorpay_signature }` to `POST /api/billing/verify`.
             - On verification success, alerts celebration and updates user's premium state.
    3. **Viral Referral Section ("Invite 3 Friends")**:
       - Fetches user's referral stats from `GET /api/referrals/me`.
       - Displays:
         - Share link: `https://resumatch.io/?ref={code}` with 1-click copy button.
         - WhatsApp share button opening `https://api.whatsapp.com/send?text=...`.
         - Progress tracker: Visual step indicator showing `{count}/3 friends joined`.
         - Reward callout: *"3 friends join → unlock 7 days free Sprint Pass!"*.
