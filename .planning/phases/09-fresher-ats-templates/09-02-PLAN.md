<objective>
Build the user-facing Resume Template Picker and Multi-Format Export modal (`client/src/components/ExportResumeModal.jsx`). Allow students to choose from 4 proven fresher templates (Classic Tech, Modern Minimal, Data & Analytics, and Campus Placement), toggle between ATS-safe PDF and editable DOCX formats, and download their tailored resumes in 1 click. Connect the modal into `ResultDisplay.jsx` and `JobTailorModal.jsx`.

Purpose: Give students complete ownership of their final resume in both modern ATS-ready PDF and editable Word formats for college placement portals.
Output: Template picker modal, download pipeline, results display integration, and Vitest test suite.
</objective>
