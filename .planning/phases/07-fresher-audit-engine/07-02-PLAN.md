<objective>
Build the user-facing Fresher Audit Dashboard (`client/src/components/FresherAuditPanel.jsx`) that translates raw diagnostic output into an intuitive, high-agency checklist for students: showing their Fresher Readiness Score, grading their personal/academic projects, pointing out missing metrics, highlighting passive words ("worked on") with 1-click active replacements, and verifying 1-page ATS layout compliance. Embed the panel seamlessly into `ResultDisplay.jsx`, `LandingPage.jsx`, and `ScorecardModal.jsx`.

Purpose: Students need direct, plain-English guidance on what to fix before campus placement drives, rather than abstract ATS error codes.
Output: Visual interactive audit panel, updated results displays, and Vitest test coverage.
</objective>
